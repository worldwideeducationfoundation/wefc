WEF Canada

Rooted in Canada, reaching the valleys of Chitral

Worldwide Education Foundation Canada raises awareness, mobilizes community support, and channels funds directly into early childhood centers, teacher training, and scholarships across Central and South Asia — where the need is greatest.

Our Programs

Three pathways out of generational poverty

From a child's first classroom to a university degree abroad, we build a continuous ladder of opportunity for children and youth in underserved communities.

Launched 2025

Bright Beginnings — Early Childhood Learning Centers

A women-led social entrepreneurship model bringing quality Montessori education to children aged 3–5 across the remote valleys of Chitral, Pakistan.

  • Local women trained in Montessori pedagogy own and run each center
  • Montessori materials, child-sized furniture & smart-TV technology in every room
  • A scholarship fund keeps early learning open to low-income families
100centers today
3,000students
500centers by 2030
Launched 2025

Academic Scholarships — Rising Stars

We believe higher education is the ultimate catalyst for systemic change. Full scholarships send outstanding, civic-minded youth to world-class regional universities.

  • 52 scholars at the American University of Central Asia, Bishkek
  • 14 scholars at the University of Central Asia, Khorog, Tajikistan
  • Fully funded and mentored to become community change-makers
66+scholars supported

Since 2021

Teacher Training & Language Labs

Technology-enabled English language labs and rigorous teacher training strengthen classrooms and build lasting, community-rooted capacity across Upper Chitral.

  • Structured English speaking & listening labs for 2,000+ students
  • 300+ teachers trained in 2026, building toward 1,000 by 2030
  • Technology-assisted instruction reaches low- and no-internet settings

What guides us

Values we build every classroom on

Talent is universal, but opportunity is not. These convictions shape every center we open and every scholar we support.

Empowerment

We equip local women and educators with credentials and tools, creating sustainable livelihoods and community leadership.

Integrity

Absolute transparency. Under founder stewardship, 100% of public fundraising goes to ground-level operations.

Equity

We dismantle barriers of gender, caste, and geography so every child can learn — regardless of where they start.

Collaboration

We partner with governments, universities, and researchers to build enduring, scalable models of change.

Our Story

From a single act of generosity to 20,000+ children

What began as quiet support for a few schools grew into a foundation reaching thousands across Central and South Asia.

Swipe to follow the trail

1
Before 2009

Connie and Kamal Daya provided modest support to orphanages, hostels, and schools for many years. A 2004 trip to Asia brought them face-to-face with urgent needs — and a commitment to do more.

2
A shared vision

The Connie & Kamal Daya Gift Fund of The Dallas Foundation invited others who shared their vision to join, expanding collective support for underserved children and youth.

3
2009 · Founded

WEF officially began with a generous donation from the Daya Gift Fund, establishing a structured foundation to advance its mission and broaden support for children and youth.

4
Today

WEF has supported the poorest communities for years — more than 20,000 children and youth have benefited from its projects across underserved regions.
