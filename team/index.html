Connie Daya

Co-Founder and CFO

Connie Daya is the Co-Founder and Chief Financial Officer of the Worldwide Education Foundation (WEF). With an MBA in Healthcare Fiscal Management and a strong background in financial accounting, she brings deep expertise in financial oversight and operational management.

Co-Founder

Kamal Daya

Co-Founder and CEO

Kamal Daya is the CEO and Co-Founder of the Worldwide Education Foundation (WEF), which he established in 2009. With over four decades at New York Life Insurance Company—consistently ranking among the top advisors—he brings deep financial expertise alongside a strong commitment to philanthropy.
