Worldwide Education Foundation Expands Advisory Board to Strengthen Early Childhood Education Initiatives

USA June 5, 2026

Dear Friends and Supporters,

We are pleased to announce the expansion of Worldwide Education Foundation (WEF), USA Advisory Board — a milestone that reflects the growing momentum behind our mission to advance early childhood education across underserved communities.

What began as a grassroots initiative has evolved into a structured, community-driven program that empowers women as educators and cultivates a lifelong love of learning in young children. The addition of these new advisors strengthens our ability to deliver on that promise.

Welcoming Our New Advisors

We are honored to introduce our distinguished Advisory Board. Each brings deep expertise and a genuine commitment to our mission, and we are grateful for their voluntary leadership.

Could This Be You?

As WEF continues to grow, we are actively seeking experienced professionals in Early Childhood Development (ECD), education policy, and community-based initiatives to join our Advisory Board in a voluntary capacity.

If you or someone you know has the expertise and passion to contribute, we warmly invite you to apply or submit a nomination.

Interested in joining our Advisory Board?

We're recruiting experienced professionals in ECD, education policy, and community initiatives.
