Our Mission

nurturing minds · growing futures

Talent is universal. Opportunity is not.

We invest in the potential of children and youth across underserved communities in Central and South Asia — opening doors through early childhood development, English language learning, teacher training, research, and scholarships, so every young learner can transform their life and uplift their community.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every child, regardless of background, has access to quality education that empowers them to break the cycle of poverty and build a brighter, more prosperous future for themselves and their communities.

Our Mission

We invest in the potential of children and youth across underserved communities in Central and South Asia through early development programs, English language learning, and scholarships that open doors to opportunity and empower them to uplift their lives and communities.

Our Belief

We believe talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Every child deserves the chance to learn, grow, and thrive, regardless of financial means, caste, religion, or gender, because education unlocks human potential and creates lasting change.

Our active projects

How we turn opportunity into outcomes

From Montessori learning centers to university scholarships, every WEF program is built around one belief — that the right support, in the right place, changes a child's whole trajectory.

Launched 2025
Early Childhood

Early Childhood Learning Centers (ECLC)

A women-led social entrepreneurial model — WEF transforms dedicated spaces into technology-driven, Montessori-based learning environments.

Where

  • Private homes, community centers & government schools

Who

  • Children aged 3–5

Impact

  • 100 centers operational
  • 3,000 students
  • 200 Montessori-trained teachers & aides

Notes

  • By 2030: 500 centers, 15,000 students & 1,000 local women benefited
  • By 2035: 1,200 centers, 36,000 students & 2,400 women empowered
  • Projected investment ≈ US$4M (120 crore PKR)
Launched 2026
Research

Herzing–Daya Research & Learning Hub

One of Pakistan's first early-childhood-development research hubs in a remote mountainous region — producing locally grounded evidence, informing policy, and enabling scalable impact.

Where

  • Hosted at the University of Chitral
  • Serving a region home to 52,000 children aged 3–5
  • 40,000+ primary school students

Who

  • ECD practitioners & primary school teachers
  • Researchers & academic partners
  • Policymakers & education stakeholders

Impact

  • Smoother transition from ECD to primary education
  • A scalable, validated rural ECD model
  • Evidence informing national policy

Notes

  • Led by WEF (USA) and funded by the Herzing and Daya families
Launched 2026
Training & Skills

Herzing Training & Skills Development Program

An integrated platform for professional development, teacher training, vocational skills, and degree & diploma programs.

Where

  • Hosted at the University of Chitral & regional hubs in Lower/Upper Chitral
  • Digital & smart-TV delivery for teachers in low/no-internet settings

Who

  • Youth & job seekers
  • Educators & trainers
  • ECD practitioners & primary school teachers

Impact

  • Workforce-ready talent for high-demand sectors
  • Certification, hands-on training & job placement
  • A resource platform for scholarships, employment & entrepreneurship
  • 300+ teachers trained in 2026

Notes

  • Funded by a Herzing family grant
  • Diplomas in ECD, hospitality, travel & tourism, technology & higher-education pathways
  • By 2030: 1,000 teachers trained · By 2035: 2,400 teachers trained
  • Projected investment ≈ US$500,000 (15 crore PKR)
Launched 2025
